How it works

From a WhatsApp list to a real system

Three simple steps replace the notebook, the group chat and the "who paid?" phone calls.

Step 1

Create the group

Set the contribution, choose weekly, fortnightly or monthly payments, and pick the start date.

Step 2

Add members and turns

Members register with their email. The organiser adds them to the group and sets the turn order.

Step 3

Start the rotation

Every round and payment record is created automatically. Mark payments as they arrive and release each payout.
